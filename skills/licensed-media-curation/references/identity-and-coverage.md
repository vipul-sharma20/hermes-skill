# Exact identity and useful coverage

## Keep three identities apart

1. The literal requested label and its historical/authored/ranked meaning.
2. The modern or source-specific concept used for a photograph.
3. The organism/object actually depicted, assessed only to the evidence available.

A source can identify a species without authenticating the pictured individual.
Pixel plausibility review is not expert botanical determination.

## Supported bridges, not convenient substitutions

- Archive exact authority responses, not just an inherited `safe` flag. Inspect
  canonical/full name, authorship, rank, status, accepted record and synonym edge.
- Capitalization, spacing and punctuation may need lookup normalization; preserve
  the original label and disclose it. An EXACT flag alone may hide wrong authorship
  or rank. FUZZY, HIGHERRANK or no-match results do not repair spelling.
- Retrieve full nomenclatural records where publication/basionym/authorship matters.
  Nomenclatural registration is not accepted-taxonomy status.
- A documented abbreviation or orthographic bridge need not require an invented
  formal erratum. A genuine homonym, local numbered type, sensu qualifier, variety,
  form or hybrid needs relevant circumscription evidence instead of truncation.
- Parent-species permission does not automatically extend to a qualified form.
  Search rank-token variants deliberately; a missing `var.`/`fo.` in a query can
  hide relevant source records. Avoid rank-detection regexes whose word boundary
  after a dot incorrectly expects another word/nonword transition.
- Full descriptions, warnings, corrections and direct category membership may
  change an initial screen. State which source field supports identification;
  category evidence does not identify every neighboring plant in a mixed scene.
- A species substring inside a hybrid, competing-taxon description, distribution
  list or comparison caption is not unambiguous identification.
- A disclosed editorial nominal illustration may be defensible only when the user
  authorizes it and primary context supports it. Pin it to exact source/revision
  and disclose exclusions; do not promote it into a global synonym or field redetermination.

## Coverage is informational, not a photo quota

Read the latest gallery and its actual pixels before calling something the first
habit, flower or fruit image. Prior lease snapshots can be stale. Track mandatory
roles separately from optional geography, framing perfection or extra anatomy.

A multirole medium photograph may already communicate foliage, flowers and woody
architecture. Two complementary references can suffice; three redundant macros
may not. Don't force endless perfect-habit searches or count-building additions.
Conversely, a single flower detail may leave a real vegetative-context gap.

Strict role names remain honest: if whole-tree means complete crown and visible
base under the current contract, clipped/obscured material is medium. This does
not imply medium images are globally useless or galleries cannot be sufficient.
Use species-appropriate roles for climbers, palms, shrubs and clumping plants.
Distinguish juvenile/potted habit from mature stature.

Composites are not automatically forbidden. If full-plate reuse is permitted,
identify the target panel, state that other panels depict different subjects and
check legibility at real thumbnail/detail sizes. Do not call the full plate a
continuous photograph or silently count other panels as target coverage.

## Geography and descriptive language

- Require an actual capture statement, well-supported source title or documented
  coordinates. Native range, nationality, project affiliation, category locality
  or a geographic-looking epithet/common name do not suffice by themselves.
- Keep literal locality and evidence quote; broader country/state interpretations
  need support. A word-boundary regex fixes lexical collisions, not semantics.
- Describe visible green fruit as green; maturity/stage can be source-attributed
  when it cannot be established from pixels. Detached/placed organs stay detached.
- A hand is not calibrated scale, dusty leaves do not establish natural color, and
  a cone-like structure need not be assigned an unsupported developmental stage.
- Alt text describes visible subject, structures and setting. Put rights policy,
  sole-source rules, taxonomic debate and selection comparisons in metadata/reviews.

Closure recommendations must say what roles are sufficient, which refinements
are optional and which genuine identity/rights/usefulness blockers remain.
